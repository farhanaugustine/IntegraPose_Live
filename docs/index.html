Local by design

Your experiment stays on the device.

Models and videos are copied into private app storage for local analysis. Nothing leaves the device unless you choose to share an output.

  • No Android network permission
  • Cloud backup disabled
  • Raw live recordings capture no audio
  • User-controlled sharing and export
